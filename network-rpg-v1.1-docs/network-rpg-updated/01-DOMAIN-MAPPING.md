## 1. The Mapping Strategy

The game converts the 5 domains of the N10-009 exam into "Zones." Each zone contains a series of "Nodes" (learning modules) that must be cleared to reach the Domain Guardian.

### Mapping Table

| Game Zone | Exam Domain | Weight | Key Objective Clusters (Nodes) | Boss/Guardian |
|-----------|-------------|--------|---------------------------------|----------------|
| **Zone 1** | 1.0 Networking Concepts | 23% | OSI Model, Appliances, Cloud, Ports/Protocols, Media, Topologies, IPv4, SDN/ZTA | **The OSI Overlord** |
| **Zone 2** | 2.0 Network Implementation | 20% | Routing Tech, Switching Tech, Wireless Config, Physical Installation | **The Routing Warden** |
| **Zone 3** | 3.0 Network Operations | 19% | Org Processes, Monitoring, Disaster Recovery, IPv4/v6 Services, Management Methods | **The Sentinel of Ops** |

| **Zone 5** | 5.0 Network Troubleshooting | 24% | Troubleshooting Methodology, Cabling/Physical, Services, Performance, Tools | **The Chaos Engineer** |

## 2. Zone Breakdowns (Detailed Nodes)

### Zone 1: The Plains of Concepts
*The "Tutorial" zone. Focuses on terminology and theoretical frameworks.*

- **Node 1.1: The Seven Layers**: The OSI Model (Layer 1 $\rightarrow$ Layer 7).
- **Node 1.2: The Toolbox**: Networking appliances (Routers, Switches, Firewalls, Load Balancers, Proxies).
- **Node 1.3: The Cloud**: NFV, VPC, Service Models (SaaS/PaaS/IaaS).
- **Node 1.4: The Secret Codes**: Ports and Protocols (FTP, SSH, DNS, HTTP, etc.).
- **Node 1.5: The Physical Path**: Fiber vs. Copper, Connectors (LC, SC, RJ45).
- **Node 1.6: The Layout**: Mesh, Star, Spine-and-Leaf, North-South vs. East-West.
- **Node 1.7: The Address Book**: IPv4 Classes, Subnetting, CIDR.
- **Node 1.8: The New Wave**: SDN, SASE, Zero Trust, IPv6.

### Zone 2: The Forge of Implementation
*Hands-on configuration and deployment.*

- **Node 2.1: Pathfinding**: Static vs. Dynamic routing (BGP, OSPF, EIGRP), Metric/Administrative Distance.
- **Node 2.2: The Switchboard**: VLANs, 802.1Q, STP, Link Aggregation.
- **Node 2.3: Airwaves**: Wireless channels, WPA2/WPA3, Antennas, ESSID.
- **Node 2.4: The Physical Build**: MDF/IDF, Power (UPS/PDU), Cabling standards.

### Zone 3: The Engine Room
*Maintenance and operational excellence.*

- **Node 3.1: The Manual**: Documentation, Change Management, SLAs.
- **Node 3.2: The Watchtower**: SNMP, Syslog, SIEM, Flow data.
- **Node 3.3: The Fail-Safe**: RPO/RTO, Cold/Warm/Hot sites, High Availability.
- **Node 3.4: The Core Services**: DHCP, DNS, NTP/PTP.
- **Node 3.5: Remote Access**: VPNs (Site-to-Site, Client-to-Site), Jump Boxes.

### Zone 4: The Security Citadel
*Protection and attack mitigation.*

- **Node 4.1: The Walls**: Logical security, PKI, IAM, MFA, RADIUS/TACACS+.
- **Node 4.2: The Enemy**: DoS/DDoS, ARP Spoofing, Phishing, Malware.
- **Node 4.3: The Shield**: Device hardening, ACLs, NAC, 802.1X.

### Zone 5: The Abyss of Debugging
*The final test: finding the needle in the network haystack.*

- **Node 5.1: The Method**: Troubleshooting steps (Identify $\rightarrow$ Theory $\rightarrow$ Plan $\rightarrow$ Implement $\rightarrow$ Verify $\rightarrow$ Document).
- **Node 5.2: Physical Failures**: Cable issues, signal degradation, transceiver mismatches.
- **Node 5.3: Service Glitches**: STP loops, VLAN mismatches, DHCP exhaustion.
- **Node 5.4: The Slow-Down**: Congestion, Latency, Jitter, Packet loss.
- **Node 5.5: The Toolkit**: Nmap, tcpdump, Wireshark, Toner, Cable tester.

## 3. Mastery Requirements

To "Complete" a node and move to the next:
- **Pass Rate**: 70% correct on that node's specific question set.
- **Streak Requirement**: 3 correct answers in a row (prevents guessing).

To challenge the **Guardian**:
- All nodes in the zone must be marked "Complete."
